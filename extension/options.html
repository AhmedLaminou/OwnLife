OwnLife — YouTube time

This extension measures the minutes a YouTube video actually plays in this browser and sends them to your own OwnLife, on this computer. It sends nothing anywhere else, and does nothing on other sites.

  1. In OwnLife: Settings → Integrations → YouTube extension → Create a key.
  2. Paste the key below and press Save and test.

The server address must be one this extension may reach: http://127.0.0.1:8000 or http://localhost:8000. If OwnLife runs on another port, change host_permissions in manifest.json and reload the extension.
